Numayer is an Engineer and have achieved the Erasmus Mundus Scholarship in the Joint Master EMIMEP [(Microwave Electronics and Photonics)](https://www.erasmus-mundus.emimep.eu/) and graduating in 2026. The coordinating universities are the University of Limoges, France; University of Brescia, Italy; University of the Basque Country, Spain; and FSU Jena, Germany. Previously he graduated from [Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST)](https://aust.edu/), Bangladesh, completing B.Sc degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering with a first class result and a Dean's Honor Award.

He has been connected with research at AUST and worked as a part-time Research Assistant. He also served as a lecturer in [Dhaka International University (DIU)](https://diu.ac/), Bangladesh in 2023-24. Teaching area: Electrical Circuits, Electronics II, Electrical Machines I, Electricity and Magnetism.

His research interests include but not limited to RF and microwave technology, Telecommunication and biomedical applications, Sensors, Optoelectronics, Signal processing etc.
